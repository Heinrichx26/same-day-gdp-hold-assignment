Public data sources

1. Bureau of Transportation Statistics, Airline On-Time Performance.
   https://www.transtats.bts.gov/
   Download the monthly tables for 2024 January, February, May, June, July, and August.
   Place them as:
     data/bts/On_Time_2024_1.zip
     data/bts/On_Time_2024_2.zip
     data/bts/On_Time_2024_5.zip
     data/bts/On_Time_2024_6.zip
     data/bts/On_Time_2024_7.zip
     data/bts/On_Time_2024_8.zip

2. Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Meteorological Aerodrome Reports.
   https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/download.phtml
   Request destination stations KATL, KDFW, and KEWR for the same months.

Processed assignment tables used in the paper are in
results/liquid_replace/ and results/casa_chen.json.
Those files are derived from the public archives and do not replace the official On-Time zip files.
